# Arena Business Policy & Operating Guidelines

**Arena Name**: Champions Sports Zone  
**Arena ID**: 1  
**Location**: Downtown Sports Complex  
**Version**: 2.4 (Synthetic / Demo Business Policy)  

---

## 1. Discount Policy
* **Maximum Allowed Discount**: 20% maximum discount across all sports.
* **Non-Peak Eligibility**: Dynamic discounts of 10% or 20% are permitted exclusively during non-peak hours (06:00–16:00 Monday–Friday).
* **Price Floor**: The discounted hourly rate must never fall below the minimum price floor of ₹300/hour.
* **Stacking Restrictions**: Promotional discount codes cannot be combined with dynamic discount pricing.

## 2. Peak-Hour Restrictions
* **No Peak Discounts**: Zero discounts are permitted during peak operating windows (17:00–23:00 weekdays, all day Saturday & Sunday).
* **Peak Utilization Strategy**: Natural demand is sufficient during peak hours; optimization must focus on premium pricing and targeted waitlist notifications rather than price cuts.

## 3. Customer Notification Policy
* **Targeted Notification Fee**: SMS and WhatsApp outreach campaigns incur an internal operating cost of **₹15 per slot notification batch**.
* **Audience Frequency Cap**: Customers may receive at most one promotional notification every 48 hours to prevent notification fatigue.
* **Preferred Targeting**: Notification campaigns should prioritize customers who have played the specific sport at this arena within the past 60 days.

## 4. Cancellation & Rescheduling Rules
* **Standard Cancellation Window**: Full refund if cancelled at least 4 hours prior to slot start time.
* **Late Cancellation**: 50% cancellation fee applies if cancelled within 2–4 hours of the slot.
* **No-Show Policy**: Forfeits 100% of booking fee.

## 5. Membership & Booking Terms
* **Advance Booking Limit**: General public can book up to 7 days in advance; registered league members can book up to 14 days in advance.
* **Equipment Rental**: Basic balls and bibs are included in base turf rental.
