# Arena Business Policy & Operating Guidelines

**Arena Name**: Arena Sports Hub  
**Arena ID**: 3  
**Location**: Midtown Athletic Park  
**Version**: 3.1 (Synthetic / Demo Business Policy)  

---

## 1. Discount Policy
* **Maximum Allowed Discount**: **15% maximum discount threshold**. The optimization agent may apply 10% dynamic discounts, but 20% discounts are strictly prohibited.
* **Non-Peak Restriction**: Discounts are restricted to non-peak weekday slots (07:00–16:00).
* **Price Floor**: The final hourly rate after discount must not fall below ₹350/hour.

## 2. Customer Notification Policy
* **Notification Cost**: SMS and WhatsApp outreach campaigns incur an internal fee of **₹18 per slot notification batch**.
* **Preferred Messaging**: Targeted notifications are encouraged for multi-sport players who have booked cricket or football slots.

## 3. Peak-Hour Restrictions
* **Peak Hour Integrity**: Strict prohibition on peak-hour discounting (17:00–23:00 weekdays, all day weekends).
* **High-Demand Slots**: Peak slots should rely solely on organic demand or priority notifications.

## 4. Cancellation & Membership Rules
* **Cancellation Policy**: Cancellations within 3 hours incur a 30% retention fee.
* **Corporate Bookings**: Corporate tournament slots must be booked through direct arena manager inquiry.
