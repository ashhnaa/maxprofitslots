# Arena Business Policy & Operating Guidelines

**Arena Name**: Urban Turf Arena  
**Arena ID**: 2  
**Location**: Cyber City Rooftop Complex  
**Version**: 1.8 (Synthetic / Demo Business Policy)  

---

## 1. Discount Policy
* **Maximum Allowed Discount**: **10% strict cap**. Owner policy strictly forbids 20% discounts to protect premium brand equity and margin thresholds.
* **Prohibited Interventions**: Any action proposing greater than a 10% discount (e.g. DISCOUNT_20) must be automatically rejected by the automated optimization system.
* **Price Floor**: Minimum final booking price cannot fall below ₹400/hour under any circumstance.
* **Non-Peak Only**: Price cuts are only applicable during off-peak morning and early afternoon slots.

## 2. Weekend Restrictions
* **No Weekend Discounts**: Zero discounts are permitted on Saturdays or Sundays regardless of slot time or historical utilization.
* **Weekend Policy Rationale**: High organic footfall and community weekend tournaments maintain strong baseline demand.

## 3. Customer Notification Policy
* **Bulk Notification Fee**: Outreach campaigns incur an operating cost of **₹12 per slot notification batch** via integrated bulk messaging provider.
* **Opt-In Rules**: Promotional notifications may only target players who have active booking history at Urban Turf Arena.

## 4. Peak-Hour Restrictions
* **Peak Preservation**: Peak operating hours (18:00–23:00) must maintain 100% full rack-rate pricing without promotional price reductions.

## 5. Cancellation & Rescheduling Rules
* **Cancellation Window**: Cancellations allowed up to 6 hours before slot start for full credit refund.
* **Rescheduling**: Free rescheduling allowed once up to 3 hours prior to booking.
